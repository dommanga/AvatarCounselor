Q: Tell me about a situation in which you had to participate in a project with people whose ideas differed from yours.
Um, so last semester I had a team project in my, uh, materials class, and we had to design like a small experiment together. And there was one guy who really wanted to, um, focus everything on one method, but I thought we should test two conditions, you know, to compare. So it was kind of, um, tense at first. What I did was I just made a small table, like showing what each method would cost us in time, and I shared it in our group chat so everyone could see. And then we talked about it in the next meeting and, yeah, we kind of decided together. It was okay in the end I think.
