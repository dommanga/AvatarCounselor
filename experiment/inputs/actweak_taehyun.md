Q: Tell me about a situation in which you had to participate in a project with people whose ideas differed from yours.
Ah, um, so last semester in my team project, we were, uh, making a survey study, and there was a big disagreement about, um, whether to run it online or in person. Like half the team wanted in person for, uh, better quality data, and the other half said online is faster and cheaper. It was kind of a stressful back-and-forth for a while, honestly. And, um... yeah, it sort of resolved itself eventually and we went with a mix. In the end we got over 200 responses, which was way more than we needed for the analysis.
