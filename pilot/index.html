Before you start:

  • Use Google Chrome and allow the microphone when asked.
  • Take your time — up to about 5–10 minutes per question. Nothing moves on until you press Stop, then Submit.
  • Answer in your own words. Don't use ChatGPT or other AI tools.
  • Press Re-record to redo an answer, as many times as you like.
  • No right or wrong answers — you're not being graded.
  • We save no audio; only the text of your answers is stored.
